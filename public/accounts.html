Access levels

Viewer

Can view status, known players, kits, the item catalog, and only their own activity. Cannot send commands.

Operator

Can send item, XP, and kit commands, refresh player/attachment data, and view only their own activity.

Admin

Can also manage accounts, manually save players, create/delete kits, and clear activity.

Accounts

The primary admin is controlled by the Unraid template. Additional accounts are saved in /app/data/users.json.
