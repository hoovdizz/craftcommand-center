Earn achievements in an eligible Survival world

Minecraft requires Survival mode, an online platform account, and a world that has never enabled cheats or Creative mode. Items sent by this dashboard use server commands and are for practice only—they can prevent legitimate achievement credit.

PlayStation records supported goals as PSN trophies. Other current Bedrock platforms use Microsoft-account achievements. Progress marked here is a private browser checklist and does not read or change Xbox or PSN records.
